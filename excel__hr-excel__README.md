# HR Workforce & Salary Insights

An applied Excel portfolio combining an active-workforce dashboard, employee segmentation, delivery Pivot Tables and conditional formulas, developed from an Ostad Module 07 assignment.

**Tools** — Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, IF, SUMIF, COUNTIF

[Read the original case study (PDF)](hr-excel-case-study.pdf)

## Reported findings

- Dashboard KPIs focus on 1,233 employees with Attrition = No, from a dataset of 1,470 records.
- The report states total salary of 8,424,768 and average salary of 6,832.74; units and payment period are unspecified.
- The active workforce includes 732 male and 501 female employees; Bachelor is the largest education group with 473 employees.
- Delivery analysis covers 14,026 records and delivered amount of 9,362,570.65. Milk accounts for about 74.3%; delivered amount is not automatically revenue or profit.

## Scope

Educational datasets from an Ostad Module 07 assignment. Salary units and periodicity are not established. The report flags two lower dashboard charts with sales labels that need validation before interpretation as salary trends. The source Excel workbook was not supplied.
