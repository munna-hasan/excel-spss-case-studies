# Antenatal Care Utilization Analysis

A cross-sectional study of 5,064 women in Bangladesh, exploring factors associated with adequate antenatal care through descriptive statistics, chi-square tests and binary logistic regression.

**Tools** — SPSS, Excel

[Read the original case study (PDF)](anc-case-study.pdf)

## Reported findings

- Adequate ANC utilization was reported for 40.8% of the analyzed women; 59.2% had inadequate ANC.
- Observed adequate ANC was 35.3% in the low education group and 64.5% in the high education group (chi-square 275.035; p < 0.001).
- Observed adequate ANC rates were 26.5%, 37.0% and 57.7% in the poor, middle and rich household wealth groups (chi-square 414.657; p < 0.001).
- The reported model had 67.0% classification accuracy, ROC AUC 0.696, Nagelkerke pseudo-R-squared 0.168 and Hosmer-Lemeshow p = 0.060.

## Scope

The results describe associations, not causal effects. Several categorical predictors were modeled as ordered scores; adjusted odds ratios depend on the original variable coding. Raw data and SPSS syntax were not supplied with this portfolio case study.
