# Higher Studies Abroad Awareness Survey

A cross-sectional survey of 385 PUST undergraduates across five faculties, examining study-abroad awareness, motivation, preparation, barriers and post-study intentions.

**Tools** — Microsoft Excel, IBM SPSS, Survey analysis, Cross-tabulation, Chi-square testing

[Read the original case study (PDF)](higher-studies-case-study.pdf)

## Reported findings

- General awareness was reported by 87.5% of respondents; 16.1% reported detailed knowledge.
- Reported IELTS/GRE preparation was 5.9%, 6.8%, 19.0% and 1.7% in first through fourth year respectively.
- The most frequently reported barriers were financial issues (31.2%), poor English skills (27.1%) and lack of guidance (21.9%).
- Reported chi-square p-values for association with application intent: financial issues 0.0124; lack of guidance 0.0291; unclear admissions process 0.0004; family restrictions 0.8521.
- 28.1% planned to settle abroad, while 79.7% supported national development, as described in the supplied report.

## Scope

Results are reproduced from the supplied portfolio report and graphics. Raw survey data, questionnaire, SPSS outputs and full contingency tables were not supplied for independent verification. Chi-square results describe associations, not causal effects. Motivation categories may overlap.
